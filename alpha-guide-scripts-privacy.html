Alpha Guide Scripts – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11-01-2026

Alpha Guide Scripts (“the Scripts”) are private tools that HiCommission Music uses to manage its own YouTube channel, Alpha Guide. They are not offered to the public, and the only account that signs in to them is the channel owner's own Google account.

1. What the Scripts do

  • Upload the channel's own videos and update their titles, descriptions, playlists and visibility.
  • Read the channel's own video statistics and analytics.
  • Read comments left on the channel's videos and post the channel's replies.

2. What information they use

  • Access to the Alpha Guide channel through Google's sign-in, granted by the channel owner.
  • Public comments on the channel's own videos, which are read only so the channel can reply. They are not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's computer.

3. Sharing

The Scripts do not sell, share or transfer any information to anyone. They send data only to YouTube, to carry out the actions above.

4. Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
