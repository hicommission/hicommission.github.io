Using the app

  • Play a song: tap any of the twelve songs. The frame around the band photo pulses in time with the music.
  • Move through a song: drag the slider in the bar at the bottom.
  • Loop: tap the loop button to cycle Off → Loop All (the whole album) → Loop One (the current song).
  • Lock Screen and headphones: play, pause, skip and scrub work from the Lock Screen, Control Center and headphone controls.
  • About the band: tap ⋯ (top right) → About BlaKats.
