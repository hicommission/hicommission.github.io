VideoOrganizer for iPhone and iPad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10-23-2026

VideoOrganizer (“the App”) helps you organize your own YouTube playlists on iPhone and iPad. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the iOS app does with your information. It covers the iOS app only.

1. Signing in with Google

You sign in with your Google account through Google’s own sign-in screen. The App asks Google for:

  • Access to your YouTube account (to show and manage your playlists)
  • Your basic profile (name and email address), which Google includes with every sign-in. The App does not display, store or use it.

We never see or store your Google password. Google stores the sign-in session securely in the iOS Keychain on your device. When you choose Log Out in the App, the session is removed from your device.

2. Your YouTube playlists

  • The App reads your playlists and the videos in them from the YouTube Data API.
  • When you create, rename or delete a playlist, or add, move or remove a video, the App sends that change to YouTube so it appears in your YouTube account.
  • When you paste a YouTube link to add a video, the App asks YouTube for that video’s public title and thumbnail.
  • A copy of your playlist list is cached on your device so the App opens quickly. It is deleted when you log out or delete the App.

3. Dictating a playlist name (optional)

If you tap the microphone while naming a playlist, the App asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission. Your speech is converted to text by Apple’s speech recognition, on your device where your device supports it; otherwise Apple may process it on its servers under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive or store your audio.

4. Playing videos

Videos play in YouTube’s official embedded player inside the App. YouTube receives the video you play and your interactions with the player, may store cookies within the player, and may show ads in videos, under Google’s privacy policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We run no analytics, advertising or tracking of our own, and the App sends no usage data to us or to anyone else. (Apple’s on-device performance reports stay on your device.)
  • We operate no servers that receive your data, and we do not sell or share your information.
  • The App does not access your photos, camera, contacts, location or files.

6. YouTube API Services

VideoOrganizer uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

VideoOrganizer’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Deleting your data

  • Log Out removes your sign-in session and the cached playlist list from your device.
  • Deleting the App removes its data from your device.
  • To remove the App’s access to your Google account: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Playlist changes you made live in your YouTube account and are managed in YouTube.

8. Children

VideoOrganizer is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
