VideoOrganizer

Keep your YouTube playlists tidy on iPhone and iPad. Sign in with your Google account, and every change you make is saved to your YouTube account. Nothing happens unless you do it.

All your playlists in one fast list

Search across playlist names, video titles and channels. Sort by newest, oldest, title or number of videos. Create playlists (type or dictate the name), rename them, and delete them with a 10-second Undo.

Organize the videos inside

Paste a YouTube link to add a video. Long-press a video to move it to another playlist or delete it, swipe to delete, or drag to reorder. Search within a playlist.

Play, loop and slow down

Play videos in YouTube's embedded player inside the app, loop a section between two points, and change the speed from 0.25× to 2×. Copy or share any video's YouTube link.
