BlaKats: Don't Feed the Animals – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10-30-2026

BlaKats: Don't Feed the Animals (“the App”) plays the twelve songs of BlaKats' album Don't Feed the Animals, which are bundled inside the App. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the App does with your information.

1. What the App collects

Nothing. The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • The App does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or files.

2. How the App works

  • The songs are part of the App and play entirely on your device. Playing music needs no internet connection.
  • The loop setting exists only while the App is open and is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • When music is playing, iOS shows the song title on the Lock Screen and in Control Center. That information stays on your device.
  • The HiCommission.com and www.blakats.com links open those websites inside the App, using Apple's built-in browser view. Those websites are run separately from the App and may have their own privacy practices. The App itself sends nothing to them.

3. Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash and usage statistics under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not combine this with any other data.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

5. Deleting your data

Because the App gathers no information, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete. Deleting the App removes it from your device.
