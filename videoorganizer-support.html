Common questions

How do I sign in?

Tap Connect YouTube and sign in with the Google account that owns your YouTube channel. The app stays signed in until you choose Log Out in the ⋯ menu.

How do I rename, delete or move things?

  • Long-press a playlist to rename or delete it. Deletes show a 10-second Undo bar.
  • Long-press a video to move it to another playlist or delete it. You can also swipe left to delete, or long-press and drag to reorder.
  • Use the ⋯ menu to add a video (paste its YouTube link), create a playlist, or change the sort order.

Why did a change not appear on YouTube?

Every change is sent to YouTube right away. If YouTube refuses it (for example, when the daily YouTube API limit is reached), the app shows an alert and the change is not made. Pull down on your playlists to refresh.

How do I remove the app's access to my Google account?

Log out in the app, then visit https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
